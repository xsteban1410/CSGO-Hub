# Random cheats

Glowing power-ups appear around the map; grabbing one gives a random cheat for 30 s: wallhack, aimbot, infinite ammo,
speed, low gravity, god mode, invisibility, one-shot kills, autobhop, super jump or regeneration. The engine's anticheat
leaves a player alone while a power-up is on.

Settings (`--set` or your own mode): `modo_cheats_duracion`, `_cantidad`, `_reaparicion`, `_bots`, `_lista`.
